AI

With models and agents, I care about one thing: give them a clear question, and see whether they can carry it all the way to something I can use and check.

AI in context: where different threads meet

What I ask of agents

Spell out what done looks like

Before picking a method, lay out the question, the goal, the constraints, and how we’ll know it’s finished.

Keep building and reviewing separate

One side builds and pushes the work forward. The other checks the output itself against the goal, going back to primary sources and running the work.

Make it easy to pick up in a fresh session

Long tasks spill across sessions. Whoever picks it up next should immediately see the goal, the key calls already made, and what’s still open, without starting over.

Bring in other viewpoints to catch blind spots

Pit assumptions against each other, hunt for counterexamples and gaps, and settle disagreements by checking sources and tests.

Every briefing should answer a live question

Filter the noise around what I’m trying to figure out, and point me to what’s worth reading in full and which claims still need checking.

Keep ownership of my writing and judgment

Tools help gather, organize, and critique. What I claim, what backs it up, and which sentences stay are my decisions.
