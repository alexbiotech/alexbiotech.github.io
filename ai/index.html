One builds. Another opens it in Safari.

I asked one agent to build the site and another to review it independently. The first version passed its checks, but when I opened it in Safari, the word “research” was alone on a second line with empty space to its right.

The agent building the site revised the layout; the reviewer checked it again in a browser and at phone widths. I still had to look at the result and decide what I wanted visitors to see.

Sometimes I want to put the same question to Claude, GPT, Grok, and Gemini. More answers leave me with another task: finding out whether they share a source and whether they are answering the same question.
