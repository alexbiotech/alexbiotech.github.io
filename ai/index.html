AI

I use AI for analysis, coding, design, and scientific exploration. What matters to me is whether models and agents can carry a clear question through to a result I can use and inspect.

What I ask of agents

Define what done looks like

State the question, goal, constraints, and acceptance criteria before choosing a method.

Separate execution from review

Builders move the work forward. Reviewers check it against the criteria using original sources, the actual artifact, and observed behavior.

Resume work in a new session

When work spans sessions, I want whoever takes over to recover the goal, key decisions, and open questions, then build on the progress already made.

Use different perspectives to find blind spots

Compare assumptions, seek counterexamples, and find omissions. Resolve disagreements through sources and checks.

Answer a question in each briefing

Choose information around the question at hand, with sources to read closely and claims to check.

Keep ownership of the writing and judgment

Tools help organize and review. I decide which claims to make, what supports them, and which sentences to keep.
