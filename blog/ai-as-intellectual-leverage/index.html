AI as intellectual leverage

AI gives more people a practical path from an idea to something useful. The better we understand a problem, the more we can ask of the tools.

A shorter distance to making

I see AI as intellectual leverage. An idea may need analysis, code, and design before it becomes useful. Models and agents help connect these kinds of work. They let more people try making something without first mastering every skill it requires.

Start with a real need

Imagine someone trying to understand household electricity use. With monthly bills and a clear question, they can use AI to organize the records and build a chart. The purpose is to decide what deserves investigation or change. That purpose gives the work direction.

Judgment gives leverage

The bills may cover different dates. Prices or seasons may have changed. Understanding the problem helps a person notice which comparisons make sense. A working version gives that judgment something concrete to examine, so learning and making can reinforce each other.

Expand what is possible

Lower barriers change which ideas feel worth trying. The result might be a small tool that fits one person’s life. I care about that expansion of possibility: people choose the purpose and give it attention; AI helps them turn understanding into something they can use.
