AI as intellectual leverage

Judgment gives leverage its direction

Models and agents extend what one person can do. Understanding a problem helps me ask for the right work and judge what comes back.

More people can build

An idea may need analysis, code, and design before it becomes useful. AI lowers the barriers between these kinds of work. More people can begin making something without first mastering every skill it requires.

Make the idea concrete

I care about connecting analysis, building, and verification around a real need. A working first version gives me something to inspect and improve.
