AI as intellectual leverage

Making this website involves organizing material, writing, design, programming, and checking the pages in a browser. AI can help with each part. I decide what the site should say. An idea that once required crossing several technical barriers can now become a version I can try and assess.

That makes me willing to reconsider things I could not previously make. I do not have to master every tool before trying. But the better I understand the problem, the more clearly I can describe what I need and notice what is wrong with a result. Delegating some of the work still leaves me with the choice of what is worth doing.

For a tool I would use myself, I want to look at existing options first. Someone may have solved most of the problem already. AI can help me research, compare, and adapt those options; I still need to see whether the result works well in use.

I want this ability to give more ideas a chance to be tested. As I can draw on more help with analysis, programming, and design, I also want to keep developing my understanding of the problem and my judgment of the result.
