Leaving context for my future self

After a night’s sleep, I may remember a conclusion but lose the reasoning that led to it. Which approaches had I already considered? I have to work my way through them again.

I save sudden ideas on my phone. But a note that says “choose this” still doesn’t tell me the next day why I chose it.

Watching agents hand off a long task makes me want to leave tomorrow’s self a note, too. What was I thinking about? Which path had gone nowhere? Where should I try next?

Could a journal do that? I haven’t settled on a form. I want to see whether, the next morning, I can pick up the thought I left the night before.
