AI4Bio: What evidence comes after a prediction?

I want to understand where scaling improves a biological task, and how that improvement carries into measurable function and new conditions.

I once asked on X whether scaling laws still work in biology. I also wanted examples of AI making its way into industry. That broad question needs to be made more precise: are we increasing data, compute, or model capacity? Which prediction gets better? What decision changes as a result?

A biological objective needs to be described together with its conditions. Predicting a structure, improving expression, distinguishing a cell state, and producing an intended function each require a suitable readout. I am especially interested in whether an improvement on one dataset carries over to new molecules, cell states, and experimental conditions.

RNA offers a way to connect these questions to specific measurements. Sample and colleagues paired 5′ UTR sequences with ribosome-loading measurements and tested sequences designed with their model. That points to an approach I want to pursue: define the readout and its context, then ask whether a model helps choose better candidates. Paper

The experimental loop I want to build starts there. Keep a clear comparison method, test predictions on candidates and conditions held out of model development, and bring failures back into the next round. The value of data lies in its ability to distinguish explanations, reveal where a design fails, and change what we try next.

For an industry example, I want to know where AI contributed to a decision and how far the subsequent evidence has progressed. A 2025 paper on rentosertib reported a randomized, placebo-controlled phase 2a trial involving 71 participants over 12 weeks. It provides evidence from human study of the candidate. The authors also state that larger and longer trials are needed to evaluate long-term safety and efficacy. Paper

I want to assess AI4Bio through these specific tasks and chains of evidence. Scaling is an important question alongside measurement quality, the definition of the objective, and performance under new conditions. For my own research interests, the direction worth pursuing is computation that helps choose the next experiment, followed by experiments that revise the computation.
